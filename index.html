Board Engineering

ENGINEERED IN
EVERY LAYER.

The science behind our strength. Every layer has a purpose — and every layer is precision-engineered.

01 Outer Liner High-quality kraft paper providing a smooth printable surface and weather resistance.
02 Fluting Core Engineered arches that resist bending and provide crucial cushioning for contents.

Corrugated Board

Liners are bonded to both sides of the fluting, creating the corrugated board structure.
